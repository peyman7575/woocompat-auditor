# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, technical, and constructive. Focus reviews on the change, the evidence, and the impact on users.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and interactions that violate this standard, and may restrict participation when necessary to keep collaboration safe and productive.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through their GitHub profile rather than escalating sensitive details in a public issue.
